    How I use AI

    AI speeds up the routine parts of my work. Judgment, accountability and the final sign-off stay with me.

    First drafts, not final answers

    I use AI to draft summaries, clause comparisons, emails and SOP outlines, then edit and verify everything before it goes out.

    Long documents, faster

    I have AI summarize lengthy agreements and pull out parties, dates and unusual terms, then check each point against the source document.

    Trackers and templates

    I build trackers, checklists and reporting templates faster with AI, and test them on real examples before anyone relies on them.

    Guardrails first

    I keep confidential and personal data out of public tools, follow the company's approved-tool policy, and log AI errors so they get fixed.

    My rule: AI can suggest, a person checks, and a person signs off.
